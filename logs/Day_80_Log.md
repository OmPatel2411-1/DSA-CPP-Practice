## Core Learning

Today's major takeaway:

**Shortest-path problems are not always about simply minimizing the sum of edge weights.**

The problem may instead involve:

* Maximum edge cost
* Limited number of stops
* Other state constraints

The algorithm must match the exact definition of the path cost and constraints.
