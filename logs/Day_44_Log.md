## What I Learned

* BSTs maintain an ordering relationship between left and right subtrees.
* BST searching can avoid unnecessary traversal by choosing a direction based on the target value.
* Validating a BST requires checking constraints across the entire subtree, not just immediate children.
* Inorder traversal of a valid BST produces values in strictly increasing order.
* Recursive range validation is another reliable way to verify BST structure.

## Reality Check

Knowing that `left < root < right` is not enough to validate a BST.

The real constraint applies to the **entire subtree**.

This is a good example of why blindly checking local conditions can produce a solution that looks correct but fails on deeper cases.
