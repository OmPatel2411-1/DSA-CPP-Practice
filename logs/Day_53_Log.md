## What I Learned

* Prefix Sum can be combined with a Hash Map to identify subarrays with specific properties.
* Converting `0` to `-1` allows the Contiguous Array problem to be treated as a repeated Prefix Sum problem.
* When the same Prefix Sum appears again, the subarray between the two positions has a net sum of zero.
* Frequency counting is often the first step in solving Top K Frequent Elements.
* Choosing the right data structure after building the frequency map is an important part of optimizing the solution.
