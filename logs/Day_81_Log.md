## Core Learning

A graph does not always represent a physical network.

It can represent:

* Mathematical relationships
* Ratios
* Directions
* Dependencies
* State transitions

The important skill is identifying what the nodes and edges represent before choosing the traversal strategy.

## Reality Check

The goal is to understand why DFS/BFS works for these graph transformations, rather than treating every graph problem as the same traversal template.
