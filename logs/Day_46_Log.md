## What I Learned

- The diameter of a tree does not necessarily pass through the root.
- At each node, the left and right subtree heights can be used to calculate a possible diameter.
- Recursive functions can return useful information upward instead of repeatedly recalculating it.
- A tree can be checked for balance while calculating its height.
- Bottom-up recursion helps avoid unnecessary repeated work.
