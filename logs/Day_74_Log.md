## 🧠 What I Learned

* Level-order traversal can be used to identify nodes based on their depth.
* Serialization converts a tree structure into a storable/transmittable representation.
* Deserialization reconstructs the original tree from that representation.
* Null markers are important because traversal values alone may not uniquely describe the tree structure.
* Tree problems often combine a standard traversal technique with another data-structure or algorithmic idea.
