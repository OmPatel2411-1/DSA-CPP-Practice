## Key Learnings

### 1. Maximum Sum BST in Binary Tree
- Used postorder traversal to collect information from left and right subtrees.
- A subtree must satisfy BST ordering constraints to be considered valid.
- Subtree minimum, maximum, and sum help determine validity and calculate the maximum BST sum.

### 2. Maximum Product of Splitted Binary Tree
- Calculating the total tree sum is essential before evaluating splits.
- A subtree sum represents one side of a possible split.
- The product of the two resulting subtree sums is evaluated to find the maximum.
