## 🧠 What I Learned

* Preorder gives the root before its subtrees.
* Postorder gives the root after its subtrees.
* Inorder provides the boundary between the left and right subtrees.
* Recursive tree construction becomes easier when the traversal relationships are understood rather than memorized.
* The same tree can be approached through different traversal combinations.
